Where the parts of Insaniquarium Co-op come from:
  WinFish/       - not in this repository. The WinFish decompilation of Insaniquarium, with
                   the insaniquarium-mac port's changes, downloaded at build time from
                   https://github.com/Jake-Vellora/insaniquarium-mac at the commit in
                   scripts/upstream.lock; patches/winfish.patch adds this mod's changes.
  PvZ-Portable/  - not in this repository either. PvZ-Portable's SDL2/OpenGL
                   SexyAppFramework (LGPL-3.0-or-later and the PopCap Games Framework
                   License) as trimmed by the insaniquarium-mac port, downloaded from the
                   same commit; patches/pvz-portable.patch adds this mod's hooks.
Both are fetched by scripts/fetch-upstream.sh (Mac/Linux) or Build.bat (Windows).
